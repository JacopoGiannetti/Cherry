## 8. Shared lists

You can share grocery lists with **one** other person, by giving them an invite code.

While you share a space, both people can see and modify the lists in it, see each other's
chosen display name, and see which items the other has checked off — including live on the
lock screen while shopping. Your taste profile, saved recipes and scans are never shared.

You can leave a shared space at any time from Settings. If you leave, or delete your account,
the space and its lists remain with the other person and your name is removed from them; if
nobody is left, the space and its lists are deleted.
