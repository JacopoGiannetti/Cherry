Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cherry ("we", "us") collects, uses and shares information when you use Cherry at hellocherry.app (the "Service"), including when you add it to your Home Screen.

The short version. We store your account, the recipes you import and your grocery list so the Service works on all your devices. Links you paste are read by AI to write the recipe. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see your full card number. We do not sell your personal data and we show no ads inside Cherry. Only if you accept cookies, we use Meta’s tools to measure which of our ads bring people to Cherry.

1. Who we are

The data controller is Jacopo Giannetti, operating Cherry. Privacy questions: privacy@hellocherry.app. Everything else: support@hellocherry.app.

2. Information we collect

  • Account. When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address, profile photo and a Google account identifier.
  • Onboarding answers. Optional answers about where you find recipes, who you cook for, how you eat and how often you cook. We use them to tailor the Service, for example default servings.
  • Your content. The links you paste, the recipes we create from them, your favorites and your grocery list.
  • Payment. Stripe processes your payment. We receive your subscription status, plan, renewal date and a Stripe customer identifier, never your full card details.
  • Usage data. Counters of how many imports you make and what they cost us to run, used to keep the Service sustainable and to prevent abuse. Our servers also record technical logs such as IP addresses for security.

We do not use advertising trackers or sell data. Your browser stores a small amount of data locally (for example your onboarding answers before you sign in, and an offline copy of your cookbook and list).

4. AI and imported links

When you paste a TikTok or Instagram link, a third-party download provider retrieves the public post, and Google Gemini watches the video and reads the caption to write the recipe and match each step to its moment in the video. A temporary copy of the video is stored only for this analysis and deleted right after. We do not send your name, email or account identifier to the AI service. Recipe results for the same public link may be cached and reused to make imports faster.

In cook mode, the video plays in the official TikTok or Instagram player. Those players are provided by TikTok and Instagram, which may set their own cookies and collect data under their own policies.

Cookies and ad measurement

We advertise Cherry on Instagram and Facebook. To know which ads work, we use the Meta Pixel and Meta’s Conversions API, provided by Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. They run only if you tap “Accept” in the cookie banner. If you decline, nothing is sent to Meta, from your browser or from our servers.

With your consent we share with Meta: the pages you visit on hellocherry.app, when you create an account, start a checkout or subscribe (with the plan and amount), your browser and IP address, Meta’s cookies (_fbp, _fbc) and, for a subscription, your email address, country, postcode and account ID, hashed (turned into a one-way code) before they leave our servers. Meta uses this to match the subscription to an ad you saw and to measure and improve our campaigns, under its own privacy policy. The legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time: tap Cookies at the bottom of our home page, or Cookie preferences in your account.

Apart from these, we only use what the Service needs to work: staying signed in, your settings and your cookie choice, stored on your device.

6. International transfers

Our providers may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Transfers are covered by appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

7. How long we keep information

  • Account and content: as long as your account exists. You can delete any recipe at any time.
  • Billing records: as long as required by tax and accounting law.
  • Technical logs: for a limited period for security.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali). California residents have the rights granted by the CCPA/CPRA; we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Deleting your account. In Cherry, go to You → Delete account. This cancels your membership immediately and erases your account, cookbook and grocery list. It cannot be undone. You can also email privacy@hellocherry.app.

9. Children

Cherry is not directed to children and is not offered to anyone under 16, or under the minimum age of digital consent in their country where that is lower.

10. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with our providers using access controls. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. If changes are significant we will tell you in the Service. The date at the top shows the current version.
